## Isolated packaged recovery drills

An isolated browser profile was seeded with only the already-finalized public payment record. The original profile was preserved and restored after each drill. No new Bitcoin transaction was broadcast.

1. With an unavailable local ERPNext endpoint, the packaged app moved the isolated record from ready to `post_failed`. It kept the finalized evidence, left both backend IDs empty, and did not require reconciliation. The failed state survived restart. After restoring ERPNext and applying the equivalent of the Retry transition in the isolated browser state, ChainPay posted the exact payload and returned the original `BATCH-2610-1077` / `ACC-JV-2026-00791`. A read-only backend query still found one batch.
2. A GET-only Testnet4 status fixture reported the already-posted txid as pending while forwarding all other reads to the public provider. Its POST endpoint returned 403. ChainPay changed the isolated record to `reconciliation_required` and retained its finalized evidence and both backend IDs. After restoring the public provider, fresh confirmed status did not clear the manual hold. The submitted ERPNext journal remained balanced at USD 1.00 debit and credit; ChainPay did not reverse or rebroadcast it.
3. A local accounting endpoint held a POST response while the isolated record was `posting`; the app was stopped before the response. On restart with ERPNext restored, the record rehydrated as `post_failed` with the safe-to-retry message and unchanged finalized evidence. An isolated retry returned the same batch and journal IDs; the backend still had exactly one batch for the txid.

The [A3 playbook](phase-5-slice-a3-bitcoin-accounting-smoke-playbook.md) recovery and negative-state checks are now backed by these controlled packaged drills and the desktop/backend automated tests. The original smoke profile remains `posted`, confirmed, and free of a reconciliation flag.
