Do not place seed phrases, private keys, or wallet export files in this repository or the smoke notes. Keep transaction identifiers and public addresses as the evidence.

UniSat [lists Bitcoin Testnet and Bitcoin Testnet4 as separate chains](https://docs.unisat.io/developer-support/open-api-documentation/unisat-wallet/supported-chains), both with the `tBTC` unit. Its [mobile transfer instructions](https://docs.unisat.io/products/unisat-wallet/how-to-deposit-withdraw-assets) describe signing and sending, but do not document a pre-broadcast raw-hex export. Keep this wallet on Bitcoin Testnet and confirm its export capability before attempting A2; do not transfer the recovery phrase into another tool to satisfy the gate.
